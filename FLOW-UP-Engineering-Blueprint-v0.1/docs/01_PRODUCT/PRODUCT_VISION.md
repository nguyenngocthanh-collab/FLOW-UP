# Product Vision

## Vision
Help people close the gap between knowing what they want and consistently executing actions that produce measurable results.

## Initial target segment
Students, self-learners, freelancers, and individual knowledge workers managing personal goals or projects. This follows the submitted project description; the segment must still be validated through interviews and usage.

## User problem hypothesis
People can state goals but struggle to translate them into manageable actions, fit those actions into available time, and determine whether their approach is producing progress.

## Value proposition hypothesis
FLOW-UP connects:
- desired outcomes;
- milestones and hierarchy;
- measurable indicators;
- executable actions;
- schedules;
- actual progress and review.

## Positioning boundary
FLOW-UP is not attempting to reproduce every feature of ClickUp, Notion, or Asana. It focuses on a coherent personal goal-execution loop.

## Success evidence to collect
- Can a new user create a meaningful goal without assistance?
- Can they identify the next action?
- Can they schedule an action?
- Can they update actual progress?
- Can they understand whether they are on track?
- Do they return to update progress later?

Do not claim productivity improvement until measured in a suitable study.
